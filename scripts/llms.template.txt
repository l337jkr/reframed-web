# Reframed

> An iOS wellbeing and mindset training app inspired by cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) techniques. Users capture an automatic thought, see the cognitive distortion behind it named in plain language, and practise writing a balanced, evidence-tested reframe. Inside the iOS app, reflections, thought records and personality results are stored locally on the user's device — no server-side storage of personal entries and no in-app behavioural tracking. Free to download with an optional Pro subscription or lifetime purchase, published by Hexp Studios.
